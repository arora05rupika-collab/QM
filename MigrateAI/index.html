AI Training

Your local model learns from every mapping you confirm or reject

🧠 How the local AI works
📥
1. Map
Click "AI Map Fields" in any migration. The local model (all-MiniLM-L6-v2, ~80MB) matches fields using semantic similarity — no internet needed.
👍
2. Confirm / Reject
Use the thumbs up/down buttons next to each mapped field. Every click is saved as a training example.
🔄
3. Retrain
The model retrains automatically every 24h, or you can click "Train Now". After 10+ examples it learns your naming conventions.
